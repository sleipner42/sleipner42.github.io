What Splitright does

One group, one shared list of expenses. Splitright keeps track of who paid for what and works out the balances, so nobody has to rebuild the trip budget in a spreadsheet afterwards.

Create a group

Start a group for a trip, a flat share (or an uneven one), a dinner or a project.

Add expenses

Record who paid, who took part and the currency — a whole bill or one restaurant table.

See balances

Per-person balances update as expenses are added, so the picture is always current.

Settle up

Splitright suggests transfers that clear the balances, and can simplify them into fewer payments.

How the group code works

Every group has a six-character code. Share it with the people in the group and they can join from their own device — no sign-up, no email address, no password. That is also why the code matters: it is the key to the group. We explain the mechanism and its trade-offs in full in our guide to splitting expenses without making anyone sign up.

Group codes are access credentials. Anyone holding a group's code may be able to view and change that group's content. Share codes only with people you trust and treat them like a password. The full rules are in the terms and acceptable use policy.

Three ways to split

Equally
The default. Divide an expense evenly between everyone who took part.
By exact amounts
For bills where people ordered different things — enter each person's share directly.
By weighted shares
Give each person a share of the whole, such as 2:1, for costs shared unevenly on purpose, such as rent split by room size.

Settle up with fewer payments

When it is time to square up, Splitright proposes a set of transfers that brings every balance back to zero. Turn on the option to simplify transactions and it looks for a shorter chain of payments instead of everyone paying everyone. You make the payments yourself, however you normally do — Splitright just tells you the amounts.

The arithmetic behind that is worth knowing even if you never use an app for it: netting each person to a single balance is why five people who all owe each other can finish in two transfers. We work it through, with the caveats, in our guide to settling group debts in the fewest payments, and you can try it on your own numbers in our free bill split calculator, which runs entirely in your browser. For a home there is a rent split calculator too, which splits the rent equally, by room size with the shared space shared, or by income.

No account, no ads, no tracking

There is no account system in Splitright: you do not create a profile, you do not give an email address and there is no password to forget. If you want the reasoning behind that — and the arithmetic of equal, exact and weighted-share splits, rounding and settling up with the fewest payments — the guide goes through it. This release contains no ads, no analytics or tracking SDK, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

What Splitright is not

Splitright does not move money. It is not a payment app, a bank, a wallet or a financial adviser, and it does not connect to your bank or card. Balances, exchange rates and settle-up suggestions are informational — check them before paying anyone.

It is also not a backup. There is currently no automatic retention schedule, self-service deletion or export feature, so Splitright should not be your only record of shared expenses.
